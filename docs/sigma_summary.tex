\section*{The measurement}
Every number in this document is one currency: $f^\star(2\sigma)$, the
smallest fraction of a held-out class injected into a $5000$-point corpus at
which the \emph{median expected significance} of a dataset-level two-sample
test reaches $2\sigma$ against a $200$-toy null ($50$ signal toys per
fraction, add-one empirical tail, log-interpolated between grid fractions;
lower is better).  It is the discovery question asked the way an experiment
would ask it: how rare can the new class be before this pipeline sees it at
$2\sigma$?  Tests: mean min-Euclidean distance, mean min-Mahalanobis
\emph{(Maha.)}, 3-bandwidth MMD$^2$, the SparKer NP kernel ensemble, and ---
post-discovery only --- two \emph{anchor-aware} statistics that consume the
anchors the discovery loop plants: the mean of $d_{\rm seen}-d_{\rm disc}$
\emph{(Eucl.\,(anch.))} and SparKer with kernels seeded at the discovered
anchors \emph{(SparKer\,(anch.))}.

\section*{Coverage}
\emph{CIFAR-10, leakage-free}: all 8 objectives of the design cube plus 16
residual constructions, trained from random initialisation (the encoder never
saw the held-out class), 4 holdout draws; discovery mode under three loop
variants (distance pool with the 95th-percentile cut; distance and
density-ratio pools with the paper's derived label-free cut, skip-on-refuse)
and an $n_{\min}$ sweep.  \emph{Galaxy10}: 14 spaces (6 fine-tuned
objectives, 2 transductive GCD arms, 6 residual constructions) on three
pretrained backbones, 5 holdout draws each, pre-discovery; discovery mode
(density-ratio pool, derived cut, $n_{\min}{=}5$) on the three supervised
arms.  Every cell is single-seed; the campaign's variance decomposition
(seed spread an eighth of draw spread) is why intervals are over draws.
CIFAR-100 (same from-scratch lineage) is running and will be appended.

\section*{Findings}
\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{Neither the probe nor ranking AUC predicts $f^\star$.}  The
  best-probed spaces are rarely the most detectable (CIFAR: probe $0.97$
  spaces sit mid-table; NPLM-dist.\ probes at $0.79$ yet detects at
  $0.027$), so representation quality for discovery has to be measured in
  this currency, not proxied.
\item \textbf{The best anchor-blind test is dataset-dependent, but always a
  density-ratio test.}  SparKer leads on the compact CIFAR scratch heads
  (best or tied on 17/20 spaces, never censored); MMD leads on the Galaxy10
  transfer heads (essentially never censored there, while SparKer and
  Mahalanobis are censored on 1--4 of 5 draws for half the rows).
\item \textbf{The residual construction does not set dataset-level records.}
  Its one reproducible $f^\star$ win is on the SIGReg parent (CIFAR:
  $0.037\to0.022$, every draw); elsewhere it is neutral on the median but
  \emph{stabilising} --- fewer censored cells, tighter draw ranges
  (Galaxy10 concats).  Its known wins remain the probe and per-event
  currencies.
\item \textbf{Discovery pays only through the anchors it plants.}
  Anchor-blind tests \emph{degrade} after the fine-tuning loop on both
  datasets; the anchor-aware statistics convert the same loop into the best
  pipelines measured.  The loop beats the frozen space in 38/48 CIFAR
  (arm, draw, variant) cells and in all 9 Galaxy10 (backbone, arm) blocks.
\item \textbf{The pool decides, and the density-ratio pool with the derived
  cut supplies it.}  Where the distance pool gives SupCon purity $0.000$
  (and pathological anchors), the np pool gives $0.3$--$0.9$ on CIFAR and
  median purity $1.00$ on Galaxy10 --- and $f^\star$ follows.  The scorer,
  not the cut, is what makes the pool pure; the cut sets its size and, when
  the estimated novelty is below $n_{\min}$, \emph{declines} --- every
  remaining loss is such an abstention (daggers in the tables), and the one
  arm that declines everywhere (SupCon at holdout 4, all $n_{\min}$) is
  exactly the arm whose pools are irreparably impure.  The $n_{\min}$ sweep
  says: engage as small as the clustering bears ($n_{\min}{=}5$), at no
  purity cost.
\item \textbf{The chosen algorithm} (Table~\ref{tab:sigma_best}): a
  supervised contrastive space, the density-ratio pool with the derived
  label-free cut, and the anchor-aware Euclidean statistic.  It wins on
  every dataset/backbone (1.3--1.5$\times$ over the best frozen pipeline)
  and on Galaxy10/LeJEPA it reaches $f^\star{=}0.016$ against the
  transductive GCD ceiling of $0.013$ --- within $25\%$ of a method that is
  handed the whole novel class unlabelled.
\item \textbf{The holdout draw dominates the uncertainty} everywhere
  (ranges span $3$--$5\times$ within a row; the loop amplifies draw
  variance $\sim$$5\times$), so single-draw numbers are never quoted without
  their spread, and per-draw points are tabulated in full.
\end{enumerate}
